# Voice Training Surface

## Purpose and success

Operate surface for one person enrolling a voice profile for a private local assistant. The first viewport should make the next recording step obvious, show the phrase to speak, and keep saved profiles reachable.

## Form

`seed:5545599ddda2efb9` — the user chose **A voice score you can follow** from the assigned direction card. The user also pinned a light-blue background, dark-purple foreground, and training-first opening.

## Grounded candidate set

1. Language-learning exercise books: repeated spoken prompts make a small practice sequence immediately familiar; pale blue paper, dark purple ink, short exercise headings, and a visible progress mark.
2. Personal library indexes: profiles are named and saved for later; blue index fields, dark-violet type, concise catalog density, and a clear active-entry mark.
3. Community choir rehearsal books: a voice is practiced phrase by phrase; pale blue page ground, deep-purple phrase labels, generous score-line spacing, and one line that advances as samples are completed. **Assigned direction.**
4. Public wayfinding maps: a route makes ordered steps easy to scan; cool blue fields, violet route strokes, terse labels, and a continuous path between stages.
5. Nursery planting guides: profiles are built through a few careful stages; blue-green grounds, violet headings, specimen labels, and visible stage changes.
6. Personal correspondence stationery: a private voice should feel owned by one person; blue stationery, purple ink, spacious text, and a signed profile name.
7. Interactive type specimens: changes should be visible as they happen; pale grounds, deep ink, large-to-small type contrast, and a live trace that settles when recording ends.

## Direction

Translate the choir rehearsal book into a working web tool. Keep standard tabs, form fields, buttons, and familiar status labels. Use its type hierarchy, open line spacing, blue/purple palette, and one signature move: three phrase lines form a score, and the line for the current sample draws into a settled mark when the recording is saved. The metaphor never replaces the prompt text or recording controls.

### Palette translation

The chart's dark concert ink and paper become the user's requested light-blue ground and dark-purple text. Blue carries selection and progress; lilac marks the current phrase. Red and green are reserved for error and success states.

### Raises carried into the assigned direction

- **Claim and proof:** readiness labels come from actual engine and profile data.
- **Lexicon guide words:** the sample number and sample name stay together above the current prompt.
- **Single-profile focus:** the opening view gives enrollment one dominant work area and omits an unverified metric grid.
- **Live signal:** microphone activity is visible only while recording; the trace settles when recording stops.
- **Star-atlas magnitude:** current and completed sample marks have distinct sizes, without adding chart coordinates or fabricated measurements.

## First viewport

The horizontal app header places Voice Training first, followed by Console, Offline Q&A, and Settings. The training view opens with a direct heading, a three-step phrase score, one active prompt, a speaker-name field, Record Sample 1, and Reset. A narrower profile area lists saved voices and offers verification. The engine state stays readable in the header.

## Motion thesis

- **Focal moment:** after each recording, the current score line draws to its final marker and the next prompt becomes active.
- **Continuity:** moving between tabs changes the active panel without a full-page entrance sequence.
- **Feedback:** Record, disabled, recording, saved, submitting, error, and success states change immediately.
- **Budget:** no ambient particle field, continuous parallax, or repeated scroll reveals. The audio trace runs only during recording. Reduced motion replaces line drawing with an immediate state change while preserving color and labels.

## Quality bar

Every recording step is understandable without the score metaphor. Keyboard focus, microphone-denied state, loading state, empty profile list, failed enrollment, and successful enrollment remain visible and actionable. No unsupported accuracy or latency promises. The palette remains legible at desktop and narrow mobile widths.
